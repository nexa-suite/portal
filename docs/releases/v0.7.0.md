# Nexa Buyer Portal v0.7.0

## Historical version mapping

Former historical label: `v0.7.1`. Public version: `v0.7.0`. Target commit,
original chronology and release evidence were preserved during the
Owner-authorized SCM normalization on 2026-09-01.

Released: 2026-08-18

## Summary

Buyer availability and delivery-tracking stabilization.

## Highlights

- Coarse `AVAILABLE`, `UNAVAILABLE` and `UNKNOWN` catalog availability states remain server-backed.
- Buyer delivery tracking preserves client-account-safe fields and recoverable loading/error/retry states.
- Delivery labels are localized and no internal Warehouse navigation or document pages are exposed.

## Boundary

Documents, evidence files, uploads, invoices and payments are not implemented. Full Vue pixel parity is not claimed.
